Writing it is where the time goes.

Every finding keeps its sources, open to audit

Name, affiliation, and grants go in. A sourced record and a one-sentence impact summary per finding come out. A curator accepts, edits, or rejects each one. A finding with an unverified source stays in, flagged for the curator.

Searches OpenAlex, PubMed, ORCID, ClinicalTrials.gov, NIH RePORTER, and the open web.

Four impact summaries, as the curators accepted them

One per Translational Science Benefits Model domain.

Clinical

A multidrug-resistant-organism alert tool raised facility registrations from 40% to 89% across 121 Veterans Affairs facilities.

Community

A program lifted colorectal cancer screening from 13% to 20% among 1,252 patients at three Federally Qualified Health Centers.

Economic

A sickle-cell transplant program cut annual per-patient health-care costs from $64,634 to $16,281 for 16 adults.

Policy
